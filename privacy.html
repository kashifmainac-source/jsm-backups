Privacy Policy — JSM Backups

Last updated: 19 September 2026

JSM Backups is used only by Kohinoor Art Jewellers to store its own encrypted database backups in its own Google Drive account.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive permission drive.file, which lets it see only the files it creates itself. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.

What it stores

It uploads encrypted backup files and reads them back to check them and to restore from them. The files are encrypted before they leave the showroom's server and cannot be read without a key that is never sent to Google.

Sharing

It collects no data from anyone else, and shares nothing with any third party. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, or transferred.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access), or from JSM by disconnecting Google Drive.
